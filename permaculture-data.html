Design Datasets

Concept: Zoning energy and activity from Zone 0 (home base) to Zone 5 (wild, untamed forest) [7].

Goal: Reducing energy expenditures and creating harmonious placement of human designs relative to natural flows.

Focus: Logging solar capture, passive heating vectors, windward barriers, and rainwater catchment volumes.

Trial Site: Adapting South American dry-season water management layouts to Welsh rain regimes.

Self-Reliance Data Systems

Tracking Yields & Systemic Balances

Permaculture is an ecological design framework rooted in systemic, self-governing frameworks—principles that align deeply with non-hierarchical, mutual aid organizing [7]. In designing our systems, we move away from industrial reliance and toward complete energy and food self-reliance.

Our data-gathering models track the flows of wind, solar energy, water retention, and biological inputs across our nurseries [7]. Rather than maximizing a single crop yield at the expense of local soil, permaculture maps inter-dependent relationships to build resilient food and resource webs.

Active Self-Reliance

"By mapping energy and resources, we model mutual aid in ecosystems. This design allows community groups to organize and establish local, autonomous hubs resilient to systemic ecological crises [7]."
