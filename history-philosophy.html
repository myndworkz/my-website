Our Origins

A Journey of Thought and Location

The history of our philosophy circle is inextricably linked to our physical travels [1, 3]. Our journey began in Switzerland, hosting discussion groups in Geneva [3, 7]. There, we were inspired by international perspectives and local grassroots organizers, exploring deep questions about ethics, human rights, and the ecological crisis.

About a year ago, when we returned to London [3], we decided to take these concepts further [7]. We stripped away academic jargon and formal structures, moving our sessions to local pubs where dialogue could flow organically over a pint [7, 10].

Bridging to Wales

"Now, as we prepare for our trial at the Centre for Alternative Technology in Machynlleth [1], we are expanding our vision to Welsh soil. We are pairing Natural Philosophy with hands-on Natural Forestry, bridging radical theory with direct ecological practice [9]."
