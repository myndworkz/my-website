Core Concepts

Method: Our meetings reject standard lectures. Instead, we use cooperative, free-flowing Socratic questioning [7].

Goal: Demystifying dense theories, letting everyone connect big philosophical concepts to their daily lives and local environment.

Structure: Drawing from non-hierarchical community organizing [7], we have no designated 'experts' or moderators. All participants share equal weight in directing our inquiries.

What is Natural Philosophy?

Cooperative Inquiry Outside the Academy

Historically, Natural Philosophy was the systematic study of nature and the physical universe [9]. Before science was partitioned into hyper-specialized academic blocks, it was a unified search for understanding, driven by observation, curiosity, and open dialogue.

We are reviving this expansive, inclusive concept [9]. Our meetings take philosophy out of elite institutions and put it back where it belongs—in public spaces like local pubs and community rooms [7, 10]. By stripping away academic gatekeeping, we provide an open, accessible platform where anyone can engage with ethics, epistemology, and politics [7, 10].

A Shared Search for Truth

In our sessions, philosophy is not an abstract intellectual exercise. It is a live, shared practice. Together, we dissect the ethical issues of the climate emergency, explore how we construct shared truths, and bridge radical theory with concrete action.
