Whether we are meeting in the creative basement of East London or just a few meters from the historical birthplace of the Social Contract in Geneva, our spaces are selected to build community, encourage direct, comfortable discourse, and provide a welcoming home for public philosophy.

Our London Home: The Hoxton Cabin

In London, we meet downstairs in the cozy, intimate basement event space of the Hoxton Cabin on Kingsland Road, Hackney [3, 9]. This creative neighborhood resource is perfectly styled to suit comfortable, low-barrier Socratic play [4, 10]. It feels authentic—a space where you can shed academic pretense, grab a drink, and dive deep into what matters [11, 12].

The atmosphere is highly relaxed: dogs are warmly welcomed, we distribute free popcorn to those settling in, and we encourage a friendly, unpretentious environment [7]. It is a genuine neighborhood space where both seasoned thinkers and curious passersby gather to discuss the questions they vote on [11, 12].

Our Geneva Hubs: MRL & Flanagan's

In Geneva, we operate across two distinct, beautiful locations that sit just steps apart, echoing a historic geographical circle [6, 8]:

  • Maison Rousseau et Littérature (MRL): We hold our Saturday morning coffee sessions inside this inspiring venue—the actual birthplace of Jean-Jacques Rousseau [6, 8, 13]. It provides a bright, literary haven where we can reflect on modern dilemmas over warm cups [13].
  • Flanagan's Pub: Located just 100 meters down the street from Rousseau's birthplace, this traditional, lively pub plays host to our spirited Wednesday evening sessions [6, 8]. It offers the perfect barstool backdrop to discuss complex ethical ideas in an active, friendly setting [6, 8].

We also occasionally meet at the cozy Café de la Place [6]. Because we keep things highly coordinated across the Alps, all of our Geneva sessions (both morning café and evening pub events) are managed directly through our single, English-language Meetup page in the sidebar [5, 6].

How to Join

There is no advance reading required, and we decide the topic together on the night [12, 14, 15]. Simply find a date that works for you in the sidebar links, reserve a spot so we can manage space with our hosts, and come ready to listen, think, and participate [14, 16].
