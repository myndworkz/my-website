The Forest Project Roots

Bringing Rewilding Lessons Home

Our rewilding dream didn't start in Wales—it was born on the other side of the Atlantic [6]. During our eight months exploring South America [1], we spent two intensive periods volunteering on ecological restoration projects in degraded rainforest zones [6]. We lived in jungles, climbed volcanoes, and worked directly with soil, seed-saving, and community tree nurseries [5, 6].

Witnessing how forests could regenerate with adaptive, close-to-nature management led us to a shared realization: we wanted to do this closer to home [6]. Bryn had always dreamed of returning to Wales [6], and its valleys, historically stripped of their native oak and birch, felt like the perfect canvas for rewilding.

The CAT Milestone

"We applied to the Centre for Alternative Technology (CAT) in Machynlleth for their volunteer woodlands and waterways team [1]. This September, we begin our 4-day trial [1]—the final hurdle to establishing our native nursery trials on Welsh soil."
