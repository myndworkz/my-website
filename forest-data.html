Open Source Field Logs

Nursery Trials & Ecological Monitoring

Documenting our volunteer forestry work and soil health trials in Wales [1, 7]. Our data-gathering process focuses on self-organization, pioneering species, and close-to-nature silviculture [1, 8].

Why Open-Source Data?

Our field logs monitor success factors across varying microclimates, species mixtures, and mycorrhizal soil inoculations [1, 6]. By recording growth rates and woodland diversity openly, we aim to provide a scalable model for community-led afforestation throughout Welsh valleys [1, 9].
