Open Dialogue Outside Academic Walls

Our sessions are non-hierarchical, public, and focused on cooperative inquiry [1]. We host structured but informal discussions on ethics, politics, and the climate emergency [1].

What to Expect

Each session begins with a brief introduction of a core philosophical problem or contemporary crisis [1]. From there, the floor is open to everyone. There are no experts, no lectures, and no academic prerequisites—just open, respectful public dialogue over a pint [1].
