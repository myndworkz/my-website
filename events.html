Our London Home: The Hoxton Cabin

In London, we meet downstairs in the intimate basement event space of the Hoxton Cabin on Kingsland Road, Hackney. This creative community resource is a great place to immerse yourself in a discussion that interrogates assumptions and challenges orthodoxies.

Hoxton Cabin attracts a wide range of people across age, gender, ethnicity and nationality. Free popcorn is available at the bar and dogs are warmly welcomed - both to the cabin and to our discussions.

Our Geneva Venues: MRL & Flanagan's

In Geneva, we operate across two distinct locations that sit just steps apart:

  • Maison Rousseau et Littérature (MRL): Saturday morning sessions are held inside this inspiring venue—the actual birthplace of Jean-Jacques Rousseau. It provides a bright, haven where we can discuss dilemmas modern and ancient over coffee/tea and pastries.
  • Flanagan's Pub: Located just 100 meters down the street from Rousseau's birthplace, this traditional Irish pub plays host to our Wednesday evening sessions. It offers the perfect backdrop to discuss complex ethical ideas in an active, friendly setting.

We also occasionally meet at the Café de la Place. All of our Geneva sessions (both morning café and evening pub events) are managed directly through our single Meetup page in the sidebar.

How to Join

There is no advance reading required, as we decide the topic together on the night. Simply find a date that works for you in the sidebar links, reserve a spot so we can manage space with our hosts, and come ready to listen, think, and participate.
