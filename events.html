Our London Home: The Hoxton Cabin

In London, we meet downstairs in the intimate basement event space of the Hoxton Cabin on Kingsland Road, Hackney. This creative community resource is a great place to immerse yourself in a discussion that interrogates assumptions and challenges orthodoxies.

Hoxton Cabin attracts a wide range of people across age, gender, ethnicity and nationality. Free popcorn is available at the bar and dogs are warmly welcomed — both to the cabin and to our discussions.

Our Geneva Venues: MRL & Flanagan's

In Geneva, we hold Saturday morning sessions at the Maison Rousseau et Littérature (MRL) and Wednesday evening pub sessions at Flanagan's / Café de la Place. Both gatherings are organized in English through the Geneva Philosophy Café network.

How to Participate

Reserve your place through the sidebar portals or simply turn up on the night. Arrive 15 minutes early to grab a drink and settle in before topic voting begins!
